How to export your Instagram data
  1. Open Instagram on your phone or desktop
  2. Go to Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions
  3. Select Export your information
  4. Click Create export
  5. Select your Instagram account
  6. Select Export to device
  7. Choose the following options:
    • Customize information: Select only Connections -> Followers and following
    • Date range: All time
    • Format: JSON
  8. Click Start export and wait for the download link (usually takes a few minutes). You'll receive an email when it's ready
  9. Download and extract the ZIP file
  10. Upload followers_1.json and following.json from the extracted folder
